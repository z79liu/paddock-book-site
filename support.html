Email thepho4life@gmail.com. Tell us your phone model and what you tapped before the problem, and we'll usually reply within two days.

Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. Open the app and add your first horse. Your records are saved on your phone.

A reminder didn't arrive

Check that notifications are allowed for Paddock Book in your phone's Settings. You can change the time reminders arrive on the Plans tab.

The farrier came on a different day

Log the visit with the real date. The next reminder is worked out from that date, so it moves automatically.

I deleted something by mistake

Tap Undo straight away. If it's too late for that, go to the Plans tab and restore one of the daily backups. Restoring replaces your current records with that day's copy.

How do I move my records to a new phone?

Your phone's own backup (iCloud or Google) includes them. With Pro, sign in under Plans, Sync and sharing, on both phones and your records and photos follow. You can also export a spreadsheet from the Plans tab at any time.

How do I share a horse with my barn manager?

With Pro, open the horse, scroll to Share, choose whether they can add records or only view, and tap Make an invite code. Send the invite; they sign in with their email and join, free. Remove them from the same place at any time.

Someone sent me an invite code

Tap the link in the invite, or open Plans, sign in under Sync and sharing, and tap Join with a code. Joining is free.

How do I delete my sync account?

Open Plans, then Sync and sharing, and tap Delete sync account. Your email address and every synced copy are removed from our servers. The records on your phone stay.

How do I cancel Paddock Book Pro?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple or Google. On iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. On Android, open the Play Store, tap your profile, then Payments & subscriptions. Your records stay when you cancel.

I bought Pro but it isn't showing

On the Plans tab, tap Restore purchases while signed in with the same Apple or Google account you paid with.
