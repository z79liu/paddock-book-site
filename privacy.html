Privacy policy

Last updated 20 October 2026

Paddock Book stores your horses' records on your phone, and you don't need an account to use the app. Your records only leave your phone if you turn on sync, which is optional.

What stays on your phone

Horses, care records, feeds, medications, contacts, costs and photos are saved in the app's own storage on your device. Unless you turn on sync, we can't see them.

Backups and export

The app saves a copy of your records on your phone once a day. Your phone's own backup (iCloud or Google) may include it. When you export a spreadsheet or PDF, you choose where it goes.

Reminders

Reminders are scheduled on your phone. Their content doesn't leave your device.

Photos

Photos you add are copied into the app's storage. The app only reads the photos you choose.

Sync and sharing (optional)

If you sign in to sync your records between devices or to share a horse, we store:

  • Your email address, to send your sign-in code and to show people you share with who shared a horse.
  • A copy of the records you sync (and, for horses you share, the records the people you share with add), including photos attached to them.

These are stored with our database provider, Supabase, and used only to sync and share them. People you share a horse with can see that horse's records and photos until you remove them. You can sign out at any time; your records stay on your phone.

To delete your sync account, tap Delete sync account under Plans in the app. That removes your email address and every synced copy from our servers straight away. The records on your phone are not touched.

Purchases

If you subscribe to Paddock Book Pro, Apple or Google processes the payment. We use RevenueCat to confirm your subscription. RevenueCat receives your purchase details and an anonymous ID created by the app. It doesn't receive your records, name or email address.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, and no tracking across other apps or websites.
  • No analytics on your records, and synced records are never used for anything but syncing and sharing.
  • We don't sell or share personal information.

Children

Paddock Book doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes its records from your phone. Copies in your phone's backup or in files you exported are removed when you delete those. If you used sync, delete your sync account in the app (Plans, then Delete sync account), or email us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above before the change reaches the app.
